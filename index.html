About CAF

Not a giant agency.
A seasoned storyteller you can call.

Cyber Athlete Films is led by producer and filmmaker Eddie Vu. After years telling stories for major television productions across the country, he built CAF to bring that same level of care and execution to Central Texas.

The goal is simple: make honest, visually powerful work that helps good people and good businesses connect with the audience they deserve.
